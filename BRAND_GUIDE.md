# COVENANT / FORM — Brand Guide

## Brand Name
**COVENANT / FORM**

## Logo Concept
A strong uppercase wordmark with a slash separating the spiritual idea (**COVENANT**) from its visible expression (**FORM**).

## Tagline
**Worn with purpose.**

## Mission
To create premium everyday clothing that carries quiet reminders of faith, identity, courage and grace into ordinary life.

## Color Palette
- Ink Black — `#111111`
- Warm Cream — `#F4F0E7`
- Sand — `#EBE4D6`
- Earth Accent — `#8D6E4A`
- White — `#FFFFFF`
- Muted Gray — `#726D64`

## Typography
- Editorial/display: Georgia
- Interface/body: Arial / Helvetica

These are intentionally system fonts so the site has no external font dependency.

## Buttons
- Uppercase
- Black background
- White text
- Thin border
- Slight vertical hover movement
- Minimal corner radius

## Photography Direction
- Editorial streetwear
- Natural movement
- Neutral architecture
- Hard daylight or soft directional studio light
- Cream, concrete, charcoal and earth tones
- Avoid church-stage imagery
- Use subtle symbolism rather than literal religious props

## Tone of Voice
- Clear
- Confident
- Thoughtful
- Scripture-rooted
- Never preachy
- Minimal
- Contemporary
