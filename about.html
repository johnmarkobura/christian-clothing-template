Our Mission

FAITH, WITHOUT THE COSTUME.

COVENANT / FORM is a fictional portfolio brand built around one question: what would Christian apparel look like if the design were as intentional as the message?

Built to make belief visible in ordinary life.

Our concept is simple: faith should shape the way we live, create, work and serve. Clothing is not the Gospel, but it can carry reminders of truth into the places we already go.

So the design language stays restrained. Scripture references are deliberate. Materials feel substantial. The pieces are intended to be worn because they are good clothes — and remembered because they carry meaning.

This project is a fictional e-commerce concept created as a web-development portfolio piece. The mission, products and testimonials are sample content.
